The Problem

The Circular Economy Fails at the Last Mile

Corporate sustainability teams have the intent. What they lack is the infrastructure. Three friction points kill every food recovery initiative before it starts.

⚖️

Liability Paralysis

Facilities managers face uncertainty around food safety law — the Good Samaritan Act, chain-of-custody requirements, and temperature compliance. One incident = reputational catastrophe. So they throw the food away instead.

Risk avoidance kills good intent
🔄

Operational Complexity

Coordinating daily pickups with fragmented local NGOs requires phone calls, availability checks, and manual scheduling. Facilities teams have zero bandwidth for this. The compactor is always easier.

$4,000 of untouched catering — compacted
📊

Data Gaps for ESG Reporting

Sustainability teams cannot quantify "avoided emissions" or "social impact" without manual spreadsheets. Without CSRD-compliant data, even companies that do donate cannot count it in their annual reports.

ESG data without audit trail = worthless
Our Solution

The EcoRelay Engine: Three Layers of Automation

We don't ask companies to try harder. We remove every point of friction — legally, operationally, and financially.

🤖

Smart-Batching AI (WasteOracle™)

Predicts surplus windows 48 hours in advance by syncing with return-to-office (RTO) badge data and event calendars. Pre-books couriers before the peak window — eliminating last-minute logistics failure.

🛡️

Liability-Shield Protocol

A digital Chain-of-Custody captures temperature logs at every handoff, generating a legally reviewed CoC document. Every seal is attorney-signed under an existing legal framework — not marketing language.

Tax-Credit Automator

Instant generation of Section 80G (India) or 501(c)(3) (US) receipts, linked to fair market value of donated weight. Verified by Big 4 partner signatory — EcoRelay is the data layer, not the tax advisor.

CSRD-Ready ESG Dashboard

Real-time metrics on meals donated, CO₂ diverted, water saved, and social impact score — audit-ready and formatted for CSRD, BRSR, and GRI reporting frameworks.
